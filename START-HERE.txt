CLASSROOM INTERACTIONS
Choose a personal practice focus in A1. Use the changing speaking jobs in B2, strategy menu in B3, evidence ranking in C2, source-collection indicators in D3, and evidence speaking turns in D4. In D6, compare your own earlier and later work in a Before / now / next panel. These fit inside the existing activity times. All choices save with your notes on this device.

Evidence indicators show whether notes are present, not whether they are accurate. With Answers off, ranking comparison feedback requires all three cards and a written reason. Use Download notes to keep your responses.

WORD HELP, READING AND MARKING
Click a dotted word to see its meaning. Readings opens the full course texts inside the lesson. Select words within one paragraph or note, then choose Highlight, Blue or Underline. Remove marks removes formatting from the selected words. In notes, Write lets you edit; See my marks shows the formatted text. Marks on words you rewrite are removed, while unchanged text keeps its marks.

My notebook contains Download marked notebook (.html), which preserves colours and underlining. Download notes (.txt) includes marked excerpts as text. Save a download before clearing browser data.

The Word game button opens optional word-matching practice.
The original YouTube video is embedded in the listening activity and Extra practice. Internet and YouTube access are required; browser or network restrictions may block playback. Use the course audio/read-aloud alternative when necessary. The original video differs from the adapted course transcript.
