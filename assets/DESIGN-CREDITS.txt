Editorial image
food-editorial.png generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool.
Prompt: Create a sophisticated editorial photographic collage illustration for an adult university academic-English lesson on food waste, food insecurity, evaluating evidence and essay planning. Wide 3:2 arrangement on warm ivory textured paper of an imperfect tomato, carrot, cut blood orange, loose grains, recycled paper and an empty ceramic plate. Tactile photography with restrained midnight-ink and vermilion cut-paper geometry and fine-line botanical annotations. Edible real food; directional studio light; no text, numbers, claims, logos or watermark. A visual prompt for why edible food is discarded.
