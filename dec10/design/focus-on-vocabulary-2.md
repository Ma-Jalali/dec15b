Some words are **inferred** from the definitions the key gives as answers. These are marked *(inf.)*. Each reading chapter teaches roughly 20–24 words. ## 3. Exercise types used in the book

| Exercise | What it does |
|---|---|
| Reading Comprehension | 3 open questions on the reading's main ideas, plus one personal-response question. |
| Reading Strategy | One academic reading skill per chapter: defining key terms (term + verb + word class + details), note tables, main idea and paragraph summaries, comparing two texts, reading graphs alongside text, linking words (similarity/opposition), text structure (cause/effect, introduction, effects), evaluating sources, argument and bias, coordinating vs subordinating clauses, outlining, critical-reading questions, cohesion types (reference and lexical), applying a text to the modern world. |
| Word Meaning A (matching sets) | In the first chapter of each unit, match target words to definitions in sets of 4–8. |
| Word Meaning A (multiple choice) | In the 2nd and 3rd chapters, choose the right meaning (a/b or a/b/c) of target words in context. |
| Word Meaning B | Either choose the everyday synonym of an academic word, gap-fill sentences with target words, or write a definition (the key gives "possible answers" in learner-dictionary style). |
| Word Families A | Complete a verb/noun/adjective/adverb table (given form bolded), or identify the suffixes that mark each word class. |
| Word Families B | Gap-fill with the correct derived form; or correct the form in sentences ("/" = already correct). |
| Word Tip | Short notes on word use, e.g. display and survey as both noun and verb. |
| Collocation | Open collocation tasks in unit-opening chapters ("answers will vary"), then gap-fill or matching of adj+noun, verb+noun and adv+adj collocations. |
| Expanding the Topic | Discussion or debate using new vocabulary; cloze passages; advantage/disadvantage classification; quizzes; a questionnaire (Ch 7). |
| Dictionary skills (strategy chapters) | Picking the right sense, reading the dictionary's codes and grammar patterns, pronunciation, word-family entries, choosing between synonyms. |
| Word-part strategies | Word roots, suffixes (-tion, -ity, -ance, -ive, -able, -ist), prefixes (over-, sub-, inter-, trans-, in-, ex-, e-) as meaning clues. |
| Guessing from context | Work out an unknown word from the text around it (e.g. disparity). |
| Word Knowledge | Mind-map associations, academic vs everyday vocabulary, frequency of occurrence, academic words in phrases, collocation-partner grids, do/make/take + academic nouns, academic verb collocations, meaning networks. |
| Vocabulary Learning Strategy Questionnaire | Learner self-survey of vocabulary-learning strategies (Ch 20). |
