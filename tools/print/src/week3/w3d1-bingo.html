Keep the chat going — useful phrases

Hold this side up while you walk. Try to use one phrase from every box.

Ask

  • Excuse me — can I ask you a quick question?
  • Have you ever …?
  • Would you like to …?

Follow up

  • Oh really? What was that like?
  • How come?
  • Tell me more about that.
  • What made you decide …?

Show interest

  • That’s interesting!
  • No way! Me too.
  • I’ve never thought about that.

Move on politely

  • Nice talking to you — I’d better find someone for the next square!
  • Thanks! See you later.

Follow-up questions — one for each kind of square

Have you ever …?When was that? · Where was it? · Would you do it again?
Do you …?How often? · Why do you do that? · Is it easy or hard?
Would you like to …?Why? · Where / which one? · What would you need to learn?
Do you think / know …?Why do you think so? · Can you give me an example?
