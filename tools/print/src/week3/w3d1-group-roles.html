DEC10 Week 3 · Day 1 · 3A Group work skills

Group role cards everyone has a job

Group
Each person takes one card and puts it on the table in front of them. After every activity, pass your card to the person on your left. Group of 5? Two people share the language monitor job.
✂ Cut along the dashed lines · 4 role cards per group
Role 1
Chair
You invite everyone to speak and keep everyone talking. Make sure every person reads or says something.
Say
  • What do you think, Omar?
  • We haven’t heard from … yet.
  • Does anyone have a different view?
Name
Role 2
Timekeeper
You watch the clock. Tell the group how much time is left, and help them finish on time.
Say
  • Two minutes left.
  • Five minutes left — let’s get ready to share.
Name
Role 3
Language monitor
You listen and tick the useful phrases people use (in the card game: O, F, N and R). Tell the group at the end.
Say
  • You used 4 phrases!
  • O? F? N? R? — you used all four!
Ticks
Role 4
Reporter
You tell the class the result of your group — short and clear. Take notes so you are ready.
Say
  • Our group found that …
  • Our move is … because …
Name
