DEC10 Week 3 · Day 3 · 12A Seminar · Part 2 discussion

Discussion roles — one job each

1

Deal the cards face down. One each. Fewer than 6? Leave out Includer, then Summariser.

2

Discuss Group 1’s two questions (10 min). Use your phrases at least twice — tick a box each time.

3

Group 1 presenter at your table: only listen and take notes. The discussion belongs to the group.

12A · Discussion role1 / 6

Starter

My job: opens each question.
  • Shall we start with the first question?
  • For me, the key issue is …
  • I’d like to begin by saying …
I used it · aim: 2+
12A · Discussion role2 / 6

Builder

My job: adds to others’ ideas.
  • Building on what … said, …
  • That’s a good point. In addition, …
  • Like you said, …
I used it · aim: 2+
12A · Discussion role3 / 6

Challenger

My job: politely disagrees.
  • I see your point, but …
  • I’m not sure I agree. In my country, …
  • That may be true for …, but what about …?
I used it · aim: 2+
12A · Discussion role4 / 6

Questioner

My job: asks for examples and reasons.
  • Could you give an example?
  • Why do you think that is?
I used it · aim: 2+
12A · Discussion role5 / 6

Includer

My job: brings quiet people in.
  • What do you think, …? We haven’t heard from you yet.
I used it · aim: 2+
12A · Discussion role6 / 6

Summariser

My job: sums up before you move on.
  • So, we seem to agree that …
  • Shall we move on to the second question?
I used it · aim: 2+
