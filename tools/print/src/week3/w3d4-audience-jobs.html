DEC10 Week 3 · Day 4 · 15A Group research summary presentations

Audience jobs — nobody just sits and watches

1

Sit with your feedback group. Take one job card each.

2

Do your job while the group presents (8–10 min). Notes go in your notebook.

3

After each presentation, pass your card to the left. New group = new job.

JOB 1
Audience job

Question master

Collect your group’s questions. Lead the vote. Hold up the C / P / AST card.
Step 2: your group reads its questions aloud and votes: on “3”, point at the best one.
Report: “In my audience job, I noticed that …”
↻ After this group: pass this card to the left.
JOB 2
Audience job

Evidence detective

Write every source you hear aloud: name + year. Was one missing?
Listen for: “According to … (2018)” · “A report by … found that …”
Step 3 — report first (30 seconds): “In my audience job, I noticed that …”
↻ After this group: pass this card to the left.
JOB 3
Audience job

Cause → effect tracker

Note one cause → effect chain for each speaker.
Listen for: “One of the main drivers of …” · “… is likely to lead to …” · “As a result, …”
Step 3 — report first (30 seconds): “In my audience job, I noticed that …”
↻ After this group: pass this card to the left.
JOB 4
Audience job

Language spotter

Listen for useful phrases: hand-overs, cause and effect, sources.
Listen for: “Now I’ll hand over to …” · “… due to …” · “To sum up, …”
Step 3 — report first (30 seconds): “In my audience job, I noticed that …”
↻ After this group: pass this card to the left.
