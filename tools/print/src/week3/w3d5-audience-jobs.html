DEC10 Week 3 · Day 5 · 15A Group research summary presentations

Audience jobs — no passive listeners

1

In your watching group, each person takes one card for Presentation 4.

2

Do your job while the group presents (8–10 min) and in the questions.

3

Presentation 5: pass your card to the person on your left.

✂
SET A · 1
Job 1

Feedback scribe

I fill in the Feedback Tool for our presenter.
Choose Yes, Mostly or Needs work. Write a short comment and one action point.
Say“… was good. To make it even better, you might …”
SET A · 2
Job 2

Language spotter

I play phrase bingo: I write the speaker’s name when I hear a phrase.
Use the bingo half-sheet. After the talk, tell your presenter which bingo phrases you heard.
Listen for“One major cause of … is …”
SET A · 3
Job 3

Evidence hunter

I note one number or fact and its source.
Example: 9% a year — LinkedIn report (Hyman & Smith, 2023)
Number / fact
Source
SET A · 4
Job 4

Question master

I stand up and ask our group’s question.
Points: clarification = 1 · probing = 2 · AST = 3. Clear and polite only!
Ask“Could you explain …?” · “Why do you think …?” · “How did you decide …?”
