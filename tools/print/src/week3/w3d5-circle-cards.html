DEC10 Week 3 · Day 5 · 21A Individual question practice

Inner circle — question cards

1

Inner circle (A) = the interlocutor. Hold this card. Ask Question 1.

2

Outer circle (B) answers for about one minute. A gives one quick tip.

3

B moves one seat to the right. A asks Question 2. After two rounds, swap cards.

✂
Q · 1
Inner circle · interlocutor

The individual questions

1 · Question about the ideas in the text

In Paragraph D, the writer says that “differentiating between green skills and green jobs is important.” Can you summarise the writer’s reason for saying this?

2 · Question about personal response

How do you think needing green skills could influence your future study plans or your career?

One quick tip: the writer’s reason clearly? Own words? Relevant? Easy to follow? Reasons and examples?

DEC10 Week 3 · Day 5 · 21A Individual question practice

Outer circle — speaker cards

1

Write key words only on your card (2 min). No full answers!

2

Answer for about one minute. Follow the answer shape.

3

Listen to the tip. Move one seat to the right.

✂
S · 1
Outer circle · speaker

Two answer shapes

1 · Ideas in the text
name the writer→the writer’s point→reason or detail→example from the text
2 · Personal response
your view→a reason→example from your life or study→link back to the text
The writer states that … because …
Personally, I think … · For example, in my course / country …
That’s an interesting question. · What I mean is …
