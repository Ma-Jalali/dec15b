DEC10 Week 3 · Day 5 · 23A Discussion skills (1) · Skill 3

Topics & group jobs

1

Turn over one topic. One student starts the discussion.

2

Others take the floor. Refer to sources and connect your ideas.

3

Round 1 (about 5 min) → reflect → Round 2: a different student starts.

✂
T1
Topic 1 · The green economy

The demand for green skills is growing, but workers are not learning these skills fast enough. What can governments or companies do to fix this problem?

T2
Topic 2 · The green economy

Green jobs require both technical skills and soft skills like creativity and adaptability. Which type of skill is more important, in your opinion?

T3
Topic 3 · The green economy

Many traditional jobs, like data scientists or managers, are becoming green. How do you think this will change the job market in the future?

T4
Topic 4 · The green economy

Having green skills makes workers more employable and helps them find better jobs. Do you think this will motivate more people to learn green skills? Why or why not?

JOB

Chair

Starts the discussion and keeps it moving.

“Let’s kick off by looking at…” · “To start us off, I’d say…”
Change jobs in round 2
JOB

Timekeeper

Says “One minute left” and stops the round.

“One minute left.”
Change jobs in round 2
JOB

Language monitor

Ticks each polite interjection and source phrase.

Listen for: “Sorry to jump in, but…” · “… talks about …”
Change jobs in round 2
JOB

Devil’s advocate

Gives the other side: “But on the other hand …”

“But on the other hand, …”
Change jobs in round 2
